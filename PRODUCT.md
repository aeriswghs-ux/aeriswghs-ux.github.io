## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
A portfolio of aerospace projects by Rithwik Krishna Gummadi and Rithika Priya Gummadi. Show the build process and make the supporting source inspectable.

## Users
User-confirmed audiences: curious students and beginners, technical reviewers and researchers, and admissions readers. Lead with the wind-tunnel work and offer both approachable overviews and direct paths to methodology, limitations, and source evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript for GitHub Pages. A TypeScript build-time generator produces real directory routes from typed content and Markdown, with native MathML rendered by KaTeX. No application server or browser framework. Preserve repository deep links, keyboard-accessible artifact tabs, research disclosures, and the optional CubeSat viewer with a static fallback. Core reading remains available without JavaScript. Search, filters, charts, and mode selection are progressive enhancements.

## Evidence on Hand
Repository-derived honeycomb vector and two slicer thumbnails. PROVENANCE.md records historical source and user-confirmed October 3 and October 13 build updates. A two-fan plexiglass tunnel is reported. The latest update describes dry ice in water at the inlet, air blown into the setup, and an outlet fan drawing air through. A stand using differential pressure, lift/drag load cells, and BME280 is unfinished; an airfoil/model-aircraft mounting model is planned. No calibration completion or new measurement records have been supplied. CubeSat remains exploratory. Credited NASA media provides educational context, not student-project evidence. No original hardware photography or formal research PDFs have been supplied.

## Brand Commitments
AeroAspirant. Both siblings and their distinct contributions remain visible. Do not invent measurements, finished studies, spacecraft missions, or testimonials.
